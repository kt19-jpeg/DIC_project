U.S. Chronic Disease Indicators


1. Dataset overview
	•	309,215 rows
	•	34 columns
	•	19 high level health Topics
	•	109 unique Questions (individual health indicators)

This is not person level data.
Each row is a reported measurement for a specific health indicator, for a certain place, time period, and demographic group.

2. Structure
The columns fall into groups:
	•	Time -> YearStart, YearEnd
	•	Geography > LocationAbbr, LocationDesc
	•	What is being measured -> Topic, Question, DataValue
	•	Uncertainty -> LowConfidenceLimit, HighConfidenceLimit
	•	Population breakdown -> Stratification columns (like sex, race, etc.)

3. Topic Distribution
Top topics by number of rows:
	•	Cardiovascular Disease (~30k rows)
	•	Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (~27k rows)
	•	Nutrition / Physical Activity (~26k rows)
	•	Health Status (~25k rows)
	•	Alcohol (~25k rows)

We probably shouldn’t analyze all 19 topics together.

Each topic measures different things with different units, so combining them would be messy and misleading.

A better idea is to focus on 1–2 related topics and go deeper.

Right now Cardiovascular Disease and Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease look interesting.
